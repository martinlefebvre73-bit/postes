Deliver all Parcel Services to the shipping dock for Wal-Mart, Staples / Bureau en gros and Bouclair. For Costco, deliver the parcels to the Customer Service desk (card renewal counter) at the front of the store.
Never Card the item, if store is closed, return to the store during operating hours whenever possible or make a second attempt the next day.
